## Rulings made while building

1. **The catch-up is a window, not a counter.** A post is tried once per calendar day (brand time zone) on days +1 to +3 after its planned day, on whichever of those days the laptop is on. After day +3 it is Missed. (A laptop that was off on day +1 still gets days +2 and +3.)
2. **Same-day lateness waits for tomorrow.** A post not sent within 15 minutes of its time becomes Late; the first try is the next calendar day, as asked.
3. **Facebook hand-overs are confirmed by the publisher** from 5 minutes after the time (`FacebookSender::status`); not public 60 minutes after the time = Failed plus a notice. The plan did not say who marks a handed-over post Published. The read fields (`is_published` for posts, `published` for videos) are not yet proven against a real post.
4. **Dev never touches Meta.** The senders refuse (PublishBlocked) before any call unless the graph is live, because Meta's own guard allows unpublished Facebook photo uploads and they would be left behind.
5. **Reels use `REELS` + `video_url` only**, the shape the rehearsal proved. `share_to_feed` is not on Meta's page; check after the first real Reel whether it also shows in the main feed.
6. **A Meta refusal during `media_publish` clears the double-post guard** (nothing was published); only a lost connection keeps it, so a retry first looks for the post on Instagram.
7. **Post now and New time clear the guard** (the owner has read the "check Instagram" message).
8. **If Facebook cannot be reached when taking a scheduled copy back**, the version stays "handed to Facebook" with a note, and the publisher tries again every pass. An old copy is never left to publish silently.
9. **Missed posts page** lists Missed, Late and Failed (a permanent error needs the owner too).
10. **Wake-up tasks:** Instagram at lead time and at post time, Facebook at post time only when it is not handed over; failed (temporary) versions at their retry time. Facebook hand-overs need no wake: confirmation happens at the next run.

## Final review (opus, 26 Nov 2026) and its fixes

The whole-branch review found 3 critical and 9 important problems; all were fixed test-first in `tests/Feature/PublishingReviewFixesTest.php` (23 tests):

- **C1 Instagram double post:** going late, catching up, giving up or being released no longer forgets that a publish may have gone out (`publish_started_at` stays; only the owner's Post now / New time clears it); a prepare with that mark first looks for the post on Instagram.
- **C2 Facebook double post:** the final feed or video call is remembered (`fb_started_at`); a retry after a lost connection looks in `published_posts` / `scheduled_posts` and adopts a match, otherwise stops with "check the Page".
- **C3 never delete a public post:** taking a Facebook copy back first asks whether it is already public once the time has come; public = kept and recorded as published; unreadable = nothing deleted.
- **I1** an unreachable Facebook is not a failed post (kept, asked again); Post now on a version that has a Facebook id checks first. **I2** the fingerprint and time Facebook holds are recorded (`fb_held`); an approved copy that no longer matches is taken back or moved; a post whose Facebook copy cannot be taken back is not deleted. **I3** running out of quick retries before the post time keeps trying every 30 minutes instead of going late; attempts restart after a success. **I4** the pop-up is started with `start /B` so it outlives the command. **I5** the idle stop waits for a running publish pass; every target is planned with the real time. **I6** only a saved container counts as prepared; leftover relay files are cleared first; relay blips are temporary. **I7** the catch-up gate is saved in UTC. **I8** Post now gets its own 15-minute window. **I9** the approval is checked again right before the final send.
- Minors fixed: photo ids overwritten on retry, a post already deleted on Facebook counts as taken back, line endings in adoption, the Missed badge counts Failed, the wake script runs only when the times changed.

## Deferred (not done in SM-010)

- **Windows unattended sleep** (I5b): a wake-up pass may be frozen when Windows sleeps again about 2 minutes after a timer wake, and the next task finds the lock held. Not fixed; the pilot on 4 Dec shows whether it happens (then: keep the laptop awake during a pass and let a locked run wait).
- Meta behaviour not provable without a live post: `is_published` / `published` for scheduled posts, rescheduling a scheduled video, whether deleting a scheduled post removes its temporary photos, `published_posts` / `scheduled_posts` fields for adoption.
- Instagram Stories are not returned by `{ig}/media`, so a story after a lost connection always needs the owner (safe).
- The output of the publisher child process started by `smm:serve` is not logged.
- Each minute's pass loads every unsent target and checks approval (fine for now; filter by time if it ever gets slow).
- Late posts get no wake task of their own; catch-up runs at 09:00, at another wake-up or when the app opens.

- A Windows pop-up was started but not seen on screen by Claude: confirm on the first real post.
- The every-minute publisher child process in `smm:serve` has no automated test (checked at the pilot).
- LinkedIn is not part of this part (waits on #10 and #19).
- Backup growth from post media (design §11) is still to measure after the first weeks of posts.
