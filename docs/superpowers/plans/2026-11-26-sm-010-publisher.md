# SM-010 Publisher Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Approved posts go out by themselves at their time on Facebook and Instagram (tracker #24), with retries, a next-day catch-up, a Missed list, Windows wake-ups and pop-ups.

**Architecture:** One command, `smm:publish-due`, walks every post target and asks a pure `TargetPlanner` what to do now (hand over to Facebook, prepare Instagram, publish, retry, catch up, give up). `FacebookSender` and `InstagramSender` do the Meta calls through `MetaGraph` (which already refuses publishing calls in Dev). Wake-ups are one-time Windows tasks that run the command; opening the app and the 09:00 daily run run it too.

**Tech Stack:** Laravel 13, Filament 5, SQLite, PHP 8.5, PowerShell 5.1 scripts, Meta Graph API v27.0 (`config('smm.meta')`), Http::fake in tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-phase1-posting-design.md` §7 "Part 3: Publisher" and §8 (approved 19 Nov 2026). Owner change, 26 Nov 2026: a post that was not sent on time is retried on each of the next three days before it goes to the Missed list (see Task 2).

## Global Constraints

- Nothing is sent unless `Approval::stillApproved($post)` is true at that moment (the gate SM-009 left for this part). A post that fails it is withdrawn from any Facebook hand-over and left for approval.
- Real publishing only from the folder named `App` (`PublishGuard`). In Dev `MetaGraph` is always dry-run: the publisher must never send a publishing call there. In dry-run the publisher sends nothing to Meta at all (no uploads either), writes "dry run: would ..." lines to the activity log and leaves target states unchanged.
- Times are stored UTC; shown in the brand's time zone (`Brand::timezone`, default `config('smm.display_timezone')`).
- Retries: for temporary errors (`MetaError::transient()`), up to 3 retries over about 15 minutes (after 1, 4 and 10 minutes). Permanent errors fail at once with `MetaError::plain()` text.
- On-time window: 15 minutes after the scheduled time. Catch-up: once per calendar day (brand time zone), on days +1, +2 and +3 after the scheduled day; after the third failed day the target is `Missed`. The owner then chooses "Post now" or a new time.
- Instagram lead time before the post time: 15 minutes for a photo or story, 30 minutes for anything with a video or a carousel.
- Facebook scheduling window: handed over only when the time is 10 minutes to 75 days ahead (Meta allows 10 minutes to 75 days for feed and photos and up to 6 months for video; 75 days is used for all, as the design says "inside the window"). Closer than 10 minutes: publish at the time like Instagram. Further than 75 days: `smm:daily` hands it over once it falls inside.
- Instagram AI label: `is_ai_generated=true` on the container (on the parent only for a carousel) when `AiLabel::applies($post)`. Facebook's caption already carries the AI line through `CaptionBuilder::for($target)`.
- Plain-language messages for the owner; no Meta jargon in notices.
- Windows PowerShell 5.1 scripts; no administrator prompt per post (the 21 Nov trial: an Interactive, WakeToRun task in `\SocialMediaManager\` is allowed; rewriting Daily is not). AC "Allow wake timers" must be Enable.
- Tests use `Http::fake`; a test needing live mode builds `new MetaGraph($secret, 'live')` and passes it in; `Http::preventStrayRequests()` is already on in `tests/TestCase.php`.
- Commit with `git commit -F <file>` (PowerShell 5.1 mangles quotes). Docs are updated in the last task; the owner approves each commit.

## Review Focus

1. **Double post after a crash** between `media_publish` and saving its result: the retry must not publish twice (Task 4).
2. **Edited, unapproved or deleted after Facebook already has it:** Facebook's scheduled copy must be withdrawn or updated, never left to publish old content (Task 3, Task 6).
3. **Two runs at once** (wake-up task plus the open app plus the daily run): only one may publish; the second does nothing (Task 5).
4. **Files left behind** in the Hostinger folder after a failure, a withdrawal or a Missed post (Task 4).
5. **Laptop off or clock jump:** a post due while the laptop was off is retried only inside the catch-up days and never silently sent after them (Task 2, Task 5).

### Task 2: TargetPlanner (the timing rules)

**Files:**
- Create: `app/Domain/Publishing/TargetPlanner.php`, `app/Domain/Publishing/Action.php` (enum)
- Test: `tests/Unit/TargetPlannerTest.php`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: enum `Action: string` = `Nothing, HandToFacebook, PublishFacebookNow, PrepareInstagram, PublishInstagram, RetryNow, CatchUp, GiveUp`; `final class TargetPlanner { public function plan(PostTarget $target, CarbonImmutable $now): Action }`; `public function leadMinutes(PostTarget $target): int` (30 when the type is a Reel, a carousel, a Facebook video, or the post has a video file; otherwise 15); `public function nextRetryAt(int $attempts, CarbonImmutable $from): ?CarbonImmutable` (after 1, 4, 10 minutes; `null` after the third retry).
- Consumes: `PostTarget` (with `post`, `state`, `attempts`, `late_days`, `next_attempt_at`, `work`), `config('smm.publishing')`.

Rules the tests pin (state `waiting` unless stated; `T` = the post's `scheduled_at`):
- Facebook, T between 10 minutes and 75 days ahead → `HandToFacebook`; T more than 75 days ahead → `Nothing`; T under 10 minutes ahead or passed within 15 minutes → `PublishFacebookNow`.
- Instagram, now ≥ T − lead and now < T → `PrepareInstagram` (once; when `work.relay` is already filled → `Nothing`); now ≥ T and prepared and within 15 minutes → `PublishInstagram`; now ≥ T and not prepared and within 15 minutes → `PrepareInstagram` (then publish in the same pass).
- `HandedToFacebook` and `Published` → `Nothing` always. `Failed` with `next_attempt_at` ≤ now and retries left → `RetryNow`; `Failed` permanent (no `next_attempt_at`) → `Nothing`.
- Past T + 15 minutes, still `waiting`/`queued`/`failed-with-retries-exhausted` → state becomes `Late` and the action is `Nothing` the same day (catch-up starts the next calendar day in the brand's zone).
- `Late`: on a later calendar day than the last attempt, with `late_days` < 3 → `CatchUp`; `late_days` already 3 and that day's attempt failed → `GiveUp`.
- Post not `Approved` → `Nothing`.

- Produces: `prepare(PostTarget $t, ?Closure $sleep = null): void` (uploads each media file to the relay, creates containers, polls until `FINISHED` using the Rehearsal timings: photo every 3 s up to 60 s, video every 10 s up to 600 s; stores `container_id` (parent) and `work.relay`, `work.children`; state `Queued`), `publish(PostTarget $t): void` (sets `work.publish_started_at` and saves BEFORE calling `{ig}/media_publish`; then stores `external_id`, reads `permalink`, state `Published`, `published_at`; then `cleanRelay`), `cleanRelay(PostTarget $t): void` (deletes every name in `work.relay`, clears it; never throws), `abandon(PostTarget $t): void` (cleanRelay, state `Waiting`, clears containers).

### Task 5: Publisher and `smm:publish-due`

**Files:**
- Create: `app/Domain/Publishing/Publisher.php`, `app/Domain/Publishing/Notifier.php`, `app/Console/Commands/PublishDueCommand.php`
- Test: `tests/Feature/PublisherTest.php`, `tests/Feature/PublishDueCommandTest.php`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `TargetPlanner`, `FacebookSender`, `InstagramSender`, `Approval::stillApproved`, `Activity::record`, `MetaGraph::mode()`.
- Produces: `Publisher::run(?CarbonImmutable $now = null): array{done: int, failed: int, late: int, missed: int}`; `Publisher::postNow(PostTarget $t, string $by): void` and `Publisher::newTime(PostTarget $t, CarbonImmutable $utc, string $by): void` (both used by the Missed list: they reset `late_days`, `attempts`, `last_error`, state `Waiting`; `newTime` goes through `Approval::reschedule`); `Notifier::published|failed|late|missed(PostTarget $t, string $text): void` (database notice via Filament `Notification::...->sendToDatabase($brand->users)`, plus the Windows pop-up of Task 7 when `PHP_OS_FAMILY === 'Windows'` and the app is not in tests); command `smm:publish-due {--now=}` printing one line per action.

Behaviour:
- One pass: take an exclusive lock on `<data>/run/publish.lock` (`flock`, non-blocking); if it is held, print "Another publish run is working" and exit 0.
- For each target of an `Approved` post (oldest `scheduled_at` first): if `! stillApproved` → `FacebookSender::withdraw` / `InstagramSender::abandon`, log "approval changed: not sent", skip. Otherwise act on `TargetPlanner::plan`.
- Dry-run (`MetaGraph::mode() !== 'live'`): log `dry run: would <action>` per target and change nothing.
- A `MetaError` that is transient: `attempts++`, state `Failed`, `next_attempt_at = nextRetryAt(...)`, `last_error = plain()`; permanent: `Failed`, no `next_attempt_at`, `Notifier::failed`. After the on-time window: state `Late`, `Notifier::late` once. `CatchUp` increments `late_days` and tries; failure on day 3 → `GiveUp` → state `Missed`, `abandon`, `Notifier::missed`. Success → `Notifier::published` (text says "late" when `late_days > 0`).
- Every attempt and result goes to the activity log with `by = 'publisher'`.
